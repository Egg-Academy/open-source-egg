# Governance

**Open Source Egg** is a community repo overseen by **the Egg Academy**. The mission is to connect the world through creativity.

## What this repo is

- A place for tools from the Egg Academy and from other contributors, when they serve that mission: saving cost, youth-sector work, useful creative tooling, business-running tools, and community work.
- Documentation and reference you can copy. **Not** a hosted service. Egg Academy does not run your copy. You deploy and operate tools in your own systems.

## What this repo is not

- A place for sensitive personal data, vetting results, ID scans, or secrets.
- A guarantee that any tool is complete, lawful for your context, or production-ready.
- Legal advice. Implementers remain responsible for the Privacy Act, vetting rules, employment law, and their own policies.

## Decisions

- **Egg Academy** owns the programme direction and can change structure, licenses, or visibility.
- **Maintainers** (people delegated by Egg Academy) review and merge pull requests to `main`.
- **Everyone** may read, clone, and fork. Only people Egg Academy has added on GitHub can open a pull request. Only maintainers merge to `main`.
- **Contributors** use pull requests after they have write access. [Join the contributors interest list](https://www.eggacademy.co.nz/open-source-egg/) to get added. That access is granted from the list. It is not instant.
- **Removing access.** The Egg Academy may remove a contributor at any time, without notice. That is most likely when someone misbehaves or acts outside these rules and the mission. The Egg Academy may also remove a contributor for any other reason.

## Visibility and licence

The repo is **public**. Egg Academy decides how each folder is licenced. Significant licence changes should be discussed in an issue before merge.

Work that is merged is shared under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`, unless that folder says otherwise. By contributing, you confirm you have the right to share that work. Other people may use, copy, and adapt it under that licence. That permission stays with the code. You do not take it back later.
