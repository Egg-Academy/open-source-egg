# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability or serious concern

Email **security@egg.ac** (or your usual Egg Academy security contact). Do not open a public issue for exploitable security bugs.

For concerns about **personal data accidentally committed** to this repo, contact us immediately so we can remove it and rotate any exposed credentials.

## Sensitive data in the repo

If you find vetting records, ID documents, or similar in history:

1. Do not spread or download more than needed to report.
2. Contact Egg Academy maintainers privately.
3. We will remove the content and follow our incident process.

## Scope

This policy covers the **open-source-egg** repository, including tools other people contribute. Your deployed copy of any tool is your responsibility.
